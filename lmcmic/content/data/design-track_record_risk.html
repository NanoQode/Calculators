Underwriting Methodology

Three Lines of Defense

Lending that has to make clear sense for both borrower and fund before any tranche is released.

01

Asset Risk

Independent, certified AACI accredited appraisals required on all collateral. Mortgages are underwritten against present liquidation value, never speculative future appreciation. Only urban centers with population 50,000+.

task_alt Max 75% LTV Strict Ceiling
02

Borrower Risk

Substantial borrower equity ("skin in the game") guarantees commitment to the property. All files undergo verified beacon score profiling, tax verification, and debt servicing reviews to ensure carrying capacity.

task_alt Verified Income & Personal Covenants
03

Exit Risk (Pre-Underwritten)

A private mortgage is a bridge, not a permanent destination. Every single advance carries a mandatory, written exit pathway—refinance to an institutional A-lender or sale—before a single dollar is advanced.

task_alt Mandatory Defined Refinance Roadmap
