# Life & Health / Living-Benefits Competitor SEO Research
**For:** instasure.ca (Canadian insurance lead-gen: instant info -> instant quotes -> instant policies; licensed advisors across Canada)
**Competitors:** policyadvisor.com, sunlife.ca (insurance + health), manulife.ca (personal insurance)
**Method:** WebSearch only (direct fetch blocked). Titles and URLs below are copied exactly from search results. Search snippets are paraphrased and are **not** guaranteed to be verbatim meta descriptions.
**Date:** 2026-10-03

> **Coverage limitation (read first):** The session-wide WebSearch cap (200/200) ran out after this agent's 36th search. Coverage:
> - **PolicyAdvisor:** about 28 searches, good coverage.
> - **Sun Life:** 8 searches. Life, critical illness (CI), health/dental, travel, guaranteed issue (GI) and calculators are covered. Disability, mortgage, newcomers and geo pages were **not** searched.
> - **Manulife (manulife.ca / manulife.com/ca):** **not searched directly.** Section 3 only lists Manulife products as they appear in PolicyAdvisor review titles. No manulife.ca URLs are listed because none were seen.
> - Everything marked **(inferred)** is analysis, not something observed.

## 0. TL;DR patterns
1. **PolicyAdvisor is the content leader.** It uses product silos (`/life-insurance/`, `/critical-illness-insurance/`, `/disability-insurance/`, `/health-insurance/`, `/mortgage-insurance/`, `/travel-insurance/`, `/visitor-insurance-canada/`, `/employee-benefits/`), a big carrier-review silo (`/insurance-companies/`), and province + city geo pages. Year-stamped "Best X" and "[Carrier] Review (2026)" titles are everywhere.
2. **PolicyAdvisor's geo footprint follows its licences.** It is licensed only in **ON, BC, AB and MB** ("Not available in other provinces"). It has no Quebec, Saskatchewan, Atlantic or territory pages. City pages were seen only for Ontario cities and Vancouver.
3. **Sun Life is the carrier with the "instant" angle.** Its direct-to-consumer products (Sun Life Go Term, Go Simplified with 3 health questions, Go Guaranteed with instant coverage, Express CI with instant online approval) sit next to evergreen explainers, "rates in Canada" pages and calculators (life, CI).
4. **Trust signals:**
   - PolicyAdvisor: LLQP-credentialed author pages, a licence disclosure, review counts, "30+ insurers".
   - Sun Life: claims-paid statistics (27,000+ claims, 99% approved, about $1.7B in 2025) and its history since 1865.
5. **Biggest gaps for instasure.ca:** non-ON/BC/AB/MB provinces (especially QC in French, SK, the Atlantic provinces), city pages outside Ontario, product-by-province matrices for CI, disability and health, standalone living-benefit calculators, and multilingual newcomer content.

## 5. Gap analysis and opportunities for instasure.ca (analysis / inferred)

### 5.1 Geographic gaps (highest confidence)
PolicyAdvisor is licensed only in ON, BC, AB and MB. Instasure, with advisors across Canada, can own:
- **Quebec, in French and English:** "assurance vie Québec", "assurance maladies graves", "assurance vie Montréal / Laval / Québec / Gatineau", "assurance invalidité". No competitor geo page was seen. Quebec also has the AMF regulator and its own carriers (Desjardins, iA, Beneva, La Capitale/SSQ, now Beneva). Needs AMF-compliant licensing.
- **Saskatchewan** (Regina, Saskatoon) and the **Atlantic provinces** (Halifax, Moncton, Saint John, Fredericton, Charlottetown, St. John's): no pages seen from any competitor searched.
- **Cities outside Ontario.** PolicyAdvisor has only Vancouver outside Ontario. Calgary, Edmonton, Surrey, Burnaby, Richmond, Kelowna, Victoria and Winnipeg are mentioned in body copy only, with no URLs. Ontario cities also missing: Markham, Vaughan, London, Kitchener-Waterloo, Windsor, Oshawa/Durham, Barrie, Oakville, Burlington.
- **Product × province matrix.** Province pages for CI, disability, mortgage protection, final expense and seniors were not seen for any competitor. Health exists only for ON/AB/BC; group benefits only for BC/AB.

### 5.2 Calculator / tool gaps
- PolicyAdvisor's needs calculator is embedded in an article and the quote flow. Sun Life has life and CI calculators.
- No competitor surfaced standalone tools for:
  - disability income replacement (per-province tax-aware)
  - mortgage protection vs term life cost comparison
  - final-expense / funeral-cost by province
  - health & dental plan cost estimator (vs provincial plan coverage such as OHIP or AHCIP)
  - an interactive cost-by-age/amount rate table
  - a "which no-medical product do I qualify for" eligibility quiz
- Each tool is a citable, linkable asset for answer engines.

### 5.3 "Instant" positioning
- Sun Life (Go suite, Express CI) and Manulife (CoverMe) own instant direct-to-consumer products.
- PolicyAdvisor already uses "Instantly" in its quote landing title.
- Gap: neutral **"instant approval life insurance Canada"**, **"buy life insurance online same day"** and **"no-medical instant decision"** comparison hubs, comparing Sun Life Go vs Manulife CoverMe vs others by issue age, max coverage, number of health questions and time to coverage. Not seen as dedicated pages.

### 5.4 Content-depth gaps (not surfaced in searches; verify before assuming they're absent)
- **Condition-specific life and CI underwriting:** cancer survivors, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, sleep apnea, depression/anxiety, cannabis use, HIV, epilepsy, Crohn's/colitis, kidney disease, stroke history, DUI/alcohol, hazardous occupations and hobbies (pilots, truckers, construction, scuba). Only diabetes, heart attack, overweight, smoking and nicotine were seen.
- **Disability by occupation:** trades, gig workers, nurses, dentists, self-employed DI. Only a general DI cluster was seen.
- **CI deep dives:** "is CI worth it", "CI for cancer", "return of premium on CI", "CI cost by age".
- **Multilingual newcomer content:** Punjabi, Hindi, Chinese, Tagalog, Arabic, Spanish, French. Sun Life has some Chinese content; PolicyAdvisor showed none. Combine with PolicyAdvisor's strong newcomer cluster (PR, work permit, visitors) in English.
- **Head-to-head comparisons:** PolicyAdvisor has "Sun Life vs Manulife / ivari" for term only. Open: CI head-to-heads (Sun Life vs Canada Life CI), personal-health head-to-heads (Blue Cross vs Sun Life PHI vs Manulife), and direct-to-consumer product head-to-heads (Sun Life Go vs CoverMe).

### 5.5 Freshness / quality gaps to exploit
- Competitor city pages carry stale years (Vancouver 2024, Ontario cities 2025).
- PolicyAdvisor has duplicate legacy-blog URLs, title bugs and inconsistent casing.
- Sun Life has many legacy Vignette duplicates indexed.
- A clean, consistently year-stamped (2026/2027), schema-rich site can compete on freshness.

### 5.6 E-E-A-T bar to match or beat
- LLQP-credentialed author bylines with `/author/` pages, plus a separate **"Reviewed by [licensed advisor, licence #, province]"**. PolicyAdvisor showed only "Written by".
- A licensing disclosure per province, with regulator links (FSRA ON, AIC AB, BCFSA BC, AMF QC, FCAA SK, the Insurance Council of Manitoba, etc.). Inferred: no competitor surfaced regulator links in snippets.
- Third-party review counts (reviews.io / Google), carrier count, carrier financial strength, and claims-paid statistics (Sun Life's 99% claims approval is the template).
- Price anchors in snippets, such as "$X/month for $Y coverage, age Z, non-smoker". Both competitors use these heavily, and answer engines quote them.
