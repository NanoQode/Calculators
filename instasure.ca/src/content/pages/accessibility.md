## Our standard

We design and test against the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA**, consistent with Ontario’s *Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act* (AODA). That includes keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient colour contrast, text alternatives for images, and forms with clear labels and error messages.
