Insurance decisions affect your family’s financial security, so we hold our content to a high standard.

## How we research

- **Primary sources first:** provincial regulators (e.g. FSRA, AMF, BCFSA), federal sources (CRA, IRCC, FCAC), public auto insurers (ICBC, MPI, SGI, SAAQ), insurer policy documents and industry data (IBC, OLHI, GIO).
- **Every guide lists its sources** at the end and links to them where possible.
- **Numbers are dated.** Prices and statistics include the date and the source or example profile they apply to.

## Who writes and reviews

- Guides are written by our editorial team and, where indicated, **reviewed by an advisor licensed** in the relevant line of business. The reviewer’s name, licence details and review date appear at the top of the guide.
- Reviewers check accuracy, not sales potential.

## Keeping content current

- We review high-traffic guides at least every six months and whenever rules change (for example Ontario’s July 2026 accident-benefits reform or Alberta’s 2027 Care-First auto model).
- The **“Updated”** date changes only when we make a substantive update.

## Corrections

Found an error? Email {email} with the page URL. We correct factual errors promptly and note significant corrections at the end of the guide.

## Independence

Insurers cannot pay for coverage in our guides. Comparisons explain trade-offs honestly, including situations where you may not need a product at all. Our [estimates](/quote/) are model-based ranges, never quotes, and are clearly labelled as such.

## Use of AI

We may use software tools to help research and draft. A human editor is responsible for every published guide, and licensed reviewers verify technical insurance content before it is marked “reviewed”.
