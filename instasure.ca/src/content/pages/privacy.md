This policy explains how {legal_entity} (“{site_name}”, “we”) handles personal information. We follow the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial laws, including Quebec’s *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (Law 25).

## What we collect

- **Estimate inputs** you enter (for example age, province, coverage amount). Estimates do not require your name or contact details.
- **Contact details** when you request a quote, a review or a saved quote: name, email, phone, postal code.
- **Sensitive information** such as smoking status or health-related answers, only when needed for the product you ask about and only with your consent.
- **Usage data:** pages visited and anonymous analytics. Persistent analytics identifiers are set only after you accept cookies.

## Why we use it

- To calculate estimates and show results.
- To connect you with **one advisor licensed in your province** for the product you asked about, and to let that advisor respond to your request.
- To send the estimate or saved quote you requested, and — **only with your express consent** — reminders and helpful information by email. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To improve the site, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

## Who we share it with

- The licensed advisor or brokerage handling your request, and — if you choose to apply — the insurer(s) needed to provide quotes or coverage.
- Service providers that host our systems or send email for us, under contracts requiring them to protect your information.
- We **never sell** personal information.

## Retention

We keep lead information for as long as needed to respond to your request and for up to 24 months afterwards for record-keeping, unless the law requires longer. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

## Your rights

You can request access to or correction of your personal information, withdraw consent, or ask us to delete it, by emailing {privacy_contact}. Quebec residents have additional rights under Law 25, including data portability and information about automated decisions.

## Cookies

We use first-party analytics (no third-party ad trackers). Persistent cookies are set only if you click **Accept** on our cookie notice. If you decline, we count page views anonymously without identifying your device.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access is limited to staff and advisors who need it to serve you.
