## Our role

{site_name} provides general insurance information, calculators and indicative estimates, and connects users with insurance advisors and brokerages **licensed in the user’s province**. General information on this site is not advice for your specific situation. Insurance products are offered only by licensed individuals and firms, in the provinces where they are licensed.

{licence_disclosure}

## Advisor licences

Each advisor’s profile lists the provinces they are licensed in, their licence type and licence number. You can verify a licence with the regulator below.

## Quebec

Online offers of insurance to Quebec residents require an AMF-registered firm. Until our partners’ registration covers Quebec, Quebec residents can use our guides and estimates and join a waitlist; we do not offer or arrange insurance for Quebec residents through this site.

## Complaints

1. Contact the advisor or brokerage, then the insurer’s complaints officer.
2. If unresolved: **General Insurance OmbudService** (home, auto, business) or the **OmbudService for Life & Health Insurance (OLHI)**.
3. You may also contact the regulator in your province.

## Estimates

Estimates are indicative ranges for the example profile and date shown. They are not quotes, offers or guarantees of coverage. Final pricing and coverage terms are set by the insurer after underwriting.
