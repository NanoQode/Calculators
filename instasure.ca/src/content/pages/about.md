## Insurance should be instant to understand

Most Canadians buy insurance a few times in their life — when they rent their first apartment, buy a car or a home, start a family or launch a business. Each time, the same questions come up: *What do I actually need? What should it cost? Who can I trust?*

**{site_name}** exists to answer those questions in minutes instead of weeks:

1. **Instant information** — plain-language guides, calculators and province-by-province rules, written for Canadians and reviewed against regulator, government and insurer sources.
2. **Instant estimates** — a realistic premium range for your profile in about 60 seconds, with no email, phone call or credit check required.
3. **Licensed advisors** — when you’re ready, we connect you with an advisor licensed in your province who compares insurers, explains the fine print and handles the application.
4. **Instant policies (coming)** — for eligible products, we are working with insurers to let you complete coverage fully online.

## What makes us different

- **Province-first.** Insurance in Canada is regulated provincially. Auto insurance alone works three different ways across the country. Every page tells you what applies where you live.
- **Transparent estimates.** Every number we show states the example profile and the date it applies to. Estimates are never presented as quotes.
- **No sales scripts.** Our goal is that you understand your options — including when you may not need more coverage.
- **Privacy by design.** Your details go only to the licensed advisor handling your request. We never sell personal information.
